## Migration `0040_ledger_payments`

Drizzle-generated from `ledger/infrastructure/postgres/schema/payments.ts`, with the hand-written rules appended below a marker in the same file (the `0039` pattern). `migrations.lock.json` and `invariants.json` are updated with it.

- `ledger.payment_attempt_status`: `INITIATED`, `PENDING`, `SUCCEEDED`, `FAILED`, `ABANDONED`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
- `ledger.payment_attempts`: trade, buyer, provider, OUR `reference` (unique, CHECKed equal to `'fund-' || id`), `provider_reference`, currency, the fee schedule version and the three amounts copied from the trade's snapshot (CHECK total = item + fee, item > 0, fee >= 0), status, the verify call's last answer (`last_provider_status`, `last_verified_at`, verified amount and currency), `journal_entry_id` (FK to the journal), `failure_reason`, and timestamps. CHECKs: `SUCCEEDED` exactly when there is a posting and a `confirmed_at`; `closed_at` exactly when `FAILED`, `ABANDONED` or `NEEDS_REVIEW`. Partial unique indexes: one open attempt (`INITIATED`/`PENDING`) per trade, one `SUCCEEDED` per trade, `(provider, provider_reference)`.
- `ledger.payment_provider_webhooks`: M8 §5.4 plus `processing_error` and `duplicate_count`; unique `(provider, provider_event_id)`; CHECK `signature_valid` (an unsigned delivery is refused, never stored); partial index on unprocessed rows.
- Hand-written: `ledger.guard_payment_attempt()` (KT012) freezes identity, amounts, `initiated_at`, and a set `provider_reference` or `journal_entry_id`; refuses `PENDING → INITIATED`; makes `SUCCEEDED`, `FAILED`, `ABANDONED` and `NEEDS_REVIEW` final in every column; stamps `updated_at`. `ledger.guard_payment_webhook()` (KT012) lets a delivery record only its processing outcome, once, and only count replays upward. `ledger.reject_delete()` (KT002) on both tables, plus `REVOKE DELETE` from `komtru_app`.

Nothing in the existing ledger tables changed.

## Shape (as built)

- **Read port** `ledger/infrastructure/postgres/tradeFunding.ts`: reads the trade's status, parties and fee snapshot by code or id, and the buyer's email, through the trades and identity barrels. Writes nothing. Takes `FOR SHARE` on the trade row inside the posting transaction, so a concurrent cancellation waits for the posting instead of interleaving. The funding gates are trades' own `readTradePrincipal` + `assertFundingEligibilityRecorded`, consumed through the barrel. **No trades-module file changed.**
- **`services/charge.service.ts`**
  - `initiateFunding`: the buyer's own trade or 404; already funded → 409 `CONFLICT`; not `PENDING_PAYMENT` → 409 `INVALID_TRANSITION`; snapshot re-checked by `fundingAmounts`; funding gates (buyer level 1, seller level 2) recorded and raised; 503 without `PAYSTACK_PUBLIC_KEY`; 422 `CONTACT_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED` without an email. A repeat returns the open attempt; two first taps race to one row on the one-open index. `restart: true` abandons an open attempt nobody is paying on (see decision 7) and issues a new reference. Returns the attempt and the Inline setup `{ key, email, amount, currency, reference, channels: ['card','ussd','bank_transfer'] }`. No provider call (design §4.4).
  - `confirmFunding`: Inline's success callback and AB4's "check again": `INITIATED → PENDING` and queue a verify. Believes nothing.
  - `paymentStatus`: AB3/AB4/V3 from the attempt via `paymentDisplayState`: `NONE`, `AWAITING_PAYMENT`, `VERIFYING`, `NOT_CONFIRMED`, `CONFIRMED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`.
- **Routes** (`http/routes.ts`, `http/webhookRoutes.ts`): `POST /trades/:code/payment/initiate`, `POST /trades/:code/payment/confirm` (both `retryable: true`, consumer scope, rate limited), `GET /trades/:code/payment/status`; `:code` takes the trade code or the trade id. `POST /webhooks/paystack` is `PUBLIC`, raw body, `x-paystack-signature`.
- **`services/webhookIngest.service.ts`**
  - `recordWebhook` (request side): signature first (401, nothing stored); `paystackEventId`; `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE duplicate_count + 1`; queue the row's job (job id = row id) when the row is new or still unprocessed; 200 regardless of enqueue failure (the sweep re-queues). A signed but unreadable body is answered 200 and not stored.
  - `settleCharge` (job side): under `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the attempt, `assessVerifiedCharge` on the verify answer; then `POST` (journal + `SUCCEEDED` + `ledger.payment.escrow_funded`, one transaction), `NEEDS_REVIEW` (close an open attempt and publish `ledger.payment.review_required`; a closed attempt is final, so the event alone records money arriving on it), `NOT_CONFIRMED` / `PENDING` (record what the provider said; `PENDING` then throws so the queue retries), or `NO_OP`. A `POST` for a trade no longer `PENDING_PAYMENT` becomes `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
  - `processDelivery`: re-parses the stored delivery; charge events are verified and settled; non-funding references are recorded as unknown without a verify call; transfer and refund events are recorded as having no handler yet (T-073, T-075).
- **Jobs** (`jobs/funding.job.ts`): `ledger.verify-charge`, `ledger.process-payment-webhook` (8 attempts, 5 s doubling, about 20 minutes, for slow transfers), and the `ledger.sweep-payment-webhooks` schedule every five minutes. `jobs/providers.ts` wires `verifyCharge`, the only place the client is imported.
- **Fee liability** is isolated in `fundingJournalLines` (`domain/funding.ts`): switching to a dedicated account is that function plus a migration for the account type.
- **Replay safety, three deep:** the dedup index; the attempt row lock and the final-status trigger; the journal key `charge:<reference>`. Tested: the same delivery twice, a second delivery for the same charge, processing every delivery twice, six concurrent verifies of one charge.
- **Stock reservation (T-056):** consumes `ledger.payment.escrow_funded` (`tradeId`); no separate event from the ledger.
- **Trade transition — later ticket.** The ledger never moves a trade. A trades subscriber to `ledger.payment.escrow_funded` must move `PENDING_PAYMENT → FUNDED` and complete the escrow action request; it is not part of T-072. That subscriber must also handle a trade cancelled or expired between the posting committing and the event arriving (the `FOR SHARE` lock only covers the posting transaction): the money is then escrowed against a trade M7 will not fund, and must be routed to a refund.

## Money rules

- **Total charged** = the trade's snapshotted `buyer_total_minor`, re-checked as item + fee; refused, never recomputed, when the snapshot is missing or does not add up.
- **Currency:** NGN only.
- **Posting at funding:** debit `PLATFORM_ESCROW_POOL` the total; credit `USER_ESCROW_LIABILITY(trade)` the item and, as a second line, the fee (decision 1). No fee line when the fee is zero. No revenue at funding.
- **Verify before posting:** only an exact match on reference, amount, currency and `success`, on an open attempt, for a trade still `PENDING_PAYMENT`.
- **Rounding:** none; integer kobo from the snapshot throughout.

## Review fixes (second review, 2026-11-07)

- **Inline amount** is the attempt's own `amount_minor`, never the fresh snapshot's. An open attempt whose amounts or fee schedule version differ from the trade's snapshot is abandoned and replaced when the grace rules allow, otherwise refused with 409.
- **Confirm and restart lock the open attempt** (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`, re-checked after the lock under READ COMMITTED), so a settle that wins the race leaves them a closed attempt to report rather than a KT012 refusal. A confirm that finds the charge already settled returns the current view and queues nothing.
- **Provider sightings and verify failures are recorded** on the attempt: `provider_seen_at` (first verify that found the charge), `last_provider_status = 'not_found'` when Paystack has no transaction with the reference (`ChargeNotFoundError` from the client), and `last_verify_error[_at]` for timeouts and 5xx. A failed verify posts nothing and leaves the delivery unprocessed.
- **Grace rules** (`domain/funding.ts`, constants): an attempt the provider never saw may be replaced, and stops showing "verifying", after `FUNDING_UNSEEN_GRACE_MS` (30 min from initiation); a declined card after `FUNDING_FAILED_GRACE_MS` (10 min); `abandoned` at once; anything the provider reports as in flight, never. At most `MAX_ATTEMPTS_PER_TRADE` (10) checkouts per trade, and `RESTART_MIN_INTERVAL_MS` (1 min) between two.
- **One review alert per attempt:** `review_published_at` is set in the transaction that publishes `ledger.payment.review_required`; the guard trigger lets a final attempt set it once and nothing else.
- **Webhook load:** the route is rate limited per IP (600 a minute). The sweep claims at most 100 deliveries per run, counts each re-queue (`sweep_count`), and marks a delivery older than 24 h or re-queued 12 times with `processing_error = 'exhausted: …'`. Exhausted deliveries stay unprocessed, so a Paystack retry of the same event still queues them and a successful run clears the mark.
- **Migration tidy-ups:** `ledger.reject_delete()` pins its `search_path`. The tests run as `komtru_app` through PgBouncer and assert it holds SELECT, INSERT and UPDATE on both tables. `grantRuntimeAccess()` re-grants DELETE on every migrate, so the `REVOKE DELETE` is belt and braces and the KT002 trigger is the guard. There are no foreign keys to `trade.trades` or `identity.users` because cross-schema references are plain uuid columns (design §4.2): each module keeps its own migrations and retention. The only foreign key is to this schema's own journal.

## Owner decisions pending

1. **Where the buyer protection fee sits between funding and release. Blocks merge.** Default implemented: a second credit line on the trade's `USER_ESCROW_LIABILITY`. The alternative is a dedicated per-trade `BUYER_PROTECTION_FEE_LIABILITY`. That needs a new enum value and a rewrite of the generated `account_key` expression (T-070: a table rewrite before PG 18), plus a change to `fundingJournalLines` only.
2. **Under-payment.** Default: `NEEDS_REVIEW`, nothing posted, and ops refunds.
3. **A reversed charge, or money on a closed or superseded attempt.** Default: review, nothing posted.
4. **Reference format.** `fund-<attempt uuid>`. Paystack refuses underscores, so the adapter doc's `trade_fund_${tradeId}` example would fail.
5. **Stock reservation.** T-056 consumes `ledger.payment.escrow_funded`.
6. **Buyers without an email.** Paystack needs one, so a phone-only buyer gets 422 `CONTACT_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`. The alternative is a platform-generated address per buyer, which means Paystack's receipts go nowhere.
7. **Paystack `failed` and `abandoned` keep the attempt open.** Inline lets a buyer retry another card against the same transaction, and a transfer can land late. Without a live account there is no proof that Inline accepts a reused reference after an abandoned checkout, so `restart` issues a fresh one under the grace rules.
8. **(a) Money for a trade no longer awaiting payment** (cancelled or expired before the posting) goes to manual review with `ledger.payment.review_required`. There is no automatic refund.
9. **(b) Restarting while the first attempt may still be paid.** This is covered by the grace rules: 30 minutes for a reference the provider never saw, 10 minutes after a decline, at once after `abandoned`, never while the provider reports the charge in flight. Money that later lands on a superseded reference goes to review once.
10. **(c) Over-payment** is held in full: the whole charge goes to review, never a partial credit with the excess refunded automatically.
11. **(d) Attempt expiry.** The default is the 30-minute provider-unseen grace from initiation, a constant in `domain/funding.ts`. The open question is whether it should instead tie to `trades.payment_deadline_hours`, and whether an attempt should close as `ABANDONED` when the trade's payment deadline passes. The latter needs the trade-side deadline job.

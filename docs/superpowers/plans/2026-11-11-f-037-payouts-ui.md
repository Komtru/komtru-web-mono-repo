# F-037 Get paid out — screens (T-124) implementation plan

**Ticket:** T-124 (`docs/tickets/13-web-app-ui.md`), feature F-037. **Screens (v2):** Seller-6, AB5, AB6, WF1, PR1.
**Branch:** `f-037-payouts-ui`. **Backend:** T-073, merged (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-11-f-037-payouts-backend.md`).
No backend change and no migration.

**Goal:** a seller sees what they can withdraw, withdraws it to a verified bank account, chooses how they are paid,
adds and manages the account money lands in, and reads every payout's outcome — success (PR1) or failure (WF1).

## Money rules (none invented here)

- Every amount is the platform's kobo digit string, formatted with `naira`/`nairaShort` (`src/trade/format.ts`).
  Nothing is summed or subtracted on the client.
- The fee is never computed: the withdraw screen states `summary.payoutFeeMinor` as the platform's figure, and the
  receipt shows the payout's own frozen `feeMinor` and `transferAmountMinor`.
- The only client checks on an amount are "a number above zero" and "not above `availableMinor`" (both against the
  platform's own figures); every other refusal (`AMOUNT_TOO_SMALL`, `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE`, `PAYOUT_HELD`,
  `PAYOUT_ACCOUNT_REQUIRED`) is the platform's and is worded by problem code.
- No arrival-time promise and no "no fee" claim: the reference's "within 2 hours, always within 24 — no fee" is not a
  platform rule. The account name is the one the bank resolved; the reference's "matched with your business
  registration" is not what T-073 does (owner decision 3), so it is not claimed.
- A withdrawal is one idempotency key per attempt the seller makes; a "try again" after a network failure reuses it.

## Routes (under Account; the ticket says Payouts lives there)

| Route                       | Screen                    | Reads / writes                                                             |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/account/payouts`          | Seller-6 + Schedule sheet | `GET /me/payouts/summary`, `GET /me/payouts`, `PUT /me/payout-preferences` |
| `/account/payouts/withdraw` | AB5                       | summary, `GET /me/payout-accounts`, `POST /me/payouts`                     |
| `/account/payouts/bank`     | AB6                       | `GET /payouts/banks`, accounts list, add / default / remove                |
| `/account/payouts/[id]`     | PR1, WF1, and in-flight   | `GET /me/payouts/:id` (polled while REQUESTED/PROCESSING)                  |

Withdraw lands on the payout's own page, which shows it on its way, then PR1 or WF1 as the platform settles it.
NEEDS_REVIEW is drawn as "being checked by our team", never as failed or paid.

## Deviations

<!-- One line per deviation the heat map shows: what differs, where, and whether it is intended.
     Everything below this comment survives the next run; everything above it is regenerated. -->

- The reference draws its own sidebar (Shop, Orders, Listings, Messages, Account) in Sell mode; the built shell is the shell's (Home, Trades, Messages, Discover, Account), owned by F-066. Not this screen's.
- **Bulk bar not built** (3 selected: Generate waybills, Mark as shipped, Message buyers), and the row checkboxes with it. No endpoint serves any of them; Park/courier waybills are out of the MVP and messaging is deferred. Owner's call whether to add bulk actions with a backend ticket.
- **Due column replaced by Next step.** The trade list carries no per-order due time; the next step is derived from status only.
- **Returns chip and the return row are not built:** nothing in the platform marks an order as a return. Chips are All, Needs action, Ready to ship, In transit, Inspecting, with live counts.
- **Create listing button omitted:** listings are deferred (F-041).
- Status chip reads the stage (Ready to ship, In transit, Awaiting buyer inspection), with the app's chip tones; 'New request' rows are not orders yet and appear on Home, not here.
- Sort is the platform's newest-activity-first, not by due time. Footer says so.

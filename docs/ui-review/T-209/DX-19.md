## Deviations

- The reference draws its own sidebar (Shop, Orders, Listings, Messages, Account) in Sell mode; the built shell is the shell's (Home, Trades, Messages, Discover, Account), owned by F-066. Not this screen's.
- Built as the existing ship form arranged in two columns: checklist and proof on the left, deadline, order and route on the right. At 1040px the screen scores about 5%, mostly the shell and type.
- **Proof of dispatch is one receipt photo plus waybill, driver phone and plate** (the platform's four required fields), not 'photos and video'.
- **'How will it travel?' shows only the seller's own arrangement.** Doorstep courier and Park hub are out of the MVP and are not drawn as options.
- 'Payment is protected' appears as the screen's subtitle and the existing 'Logistics: ready to ship' banner, not the reference's separate guard banner.
- **'Save and return to orders' is 'Back to orders'.** Nothing is saved as a draft; the form is not persisted.
- The checklist is the seller's reminder; ticking opens nothing (the guards are the evidence fields and the server).
- `/trade/[id]/dispatch` renders this workspace from 768px up and the phone's dispatch step below it, so Orders' Dispatch action reaches it.

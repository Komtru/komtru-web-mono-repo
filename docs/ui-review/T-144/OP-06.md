## Deviations

- **Shell and back row.** As OP-05: console frame instead of the reference rail and top bar; the back control is a link "People" with a text chevron where the reference has a round icon button.
- **Header.** The reference's title row "Zaza Fashion — business check" and summary panel (Case PPL-3318, What happened, Clock, Owner) become the shared `CaseLayout` header: case number, "Business registration (CAC) check", the check/user line with owner, and state/clock/priority chips. No name is available (T-050), the case number is the platform's `C-` number, and the clock is "Due in n" from the queue's due date.
- **Evidence** is the review case's recorded facts (kind of check, why it needs a person, attempts, duplicate of another account) in the Check/Result/Source table, not the reference's CAC register, director BVN, address and payout-name rows: the platform exposes no per-check results to the console yet (provider calls and merchant documents are T-154 and later). Results are plain values, not Matches/Needs-a-photo chips. The Trust Trail from the universal case sits under it, as on every case page.
- **Decision.** Advisory prose in `AdvisoryBox` with the fixed label "AI-suggested — not a decision", then the "a human decides, always" divider, as AC10. Options are Approve and Reject only; the backend has no "Ask for more information" outcome on this route (use Request evidence on the universal case). No "Add a note" button. The policy cite "Applied per M6 §6.8" is the backend's default; the analyst is not asked for a citation.
- **Take first.** The reference shows no ownership step; the backend requires the case to be taken before it is decided, so an unowned case shows "Take this case" and a case owned by someone else offers a takeover only to `cases.manage`.
- **Duplicate-flagged requests** replace the decision with "Resolve the duplicate flag" (confirm, escalate, or clear through a second approver); not in the reference.
- Permission states (no `verification.view`, view-only, no `cases.view` for the evidence) are not in the reference.

**The 560px comparison is the phone layout, not the tablet one.** The tablet reference frame is 560px wide but the app's tablet breakpoint (`md`) is 768px, so the 9.4% above compares the phone screen with the tablet drawing. The drawing was re-rendered at its declared 820px and the built screen shot at 820 × 473 (`pnpm visual-capture --width 820` sizing): **3.4% of pixels differ** after the Back link was removed from log in.

- Built as drawn: wordmark and headline over one centred 600px card; Phone number or email, Password; Log in and Create account side by side at 46px; "Have a Trade Code? Enter it here" under the card, linking to the existing `/find`.
- **Forgot password?** sits above the buttons as an underlined link; the drawing omits it, but a log-in screen without it would strand someone.
- Wordmark is the app's `KomtruLogo`, left of centre in the drawing and centred here (the drawing's own wordmark is left-aligned under a centred headline; read as a slip). Fields empty rather than filled with sample values.
- "Enter it here" goes to `/find`, which needs a session; a logged-out visitor is sent to log in and back to it. Recorded in DX-01.md.

**Live app, F-015 (2026-11-15).** Shot at `/home` with two active trades and two past ones, full page, against a scratch stand-in for the platform; the figures are fixtures, not data.

- No protected-balance hero (₦541,500 protected, ₦8.2M all-time) and no “2 active trades” hero line. The platform exposes no balance total on a list, so none is drawn. Intended.
- No greeting, for the same reason as H-03. Deviation until the viewer exposes a name.
- The Buy/Sell switch is drawn in the shell's top bar, as in the reference. The top bar's layout is the shell's own (see AC1).
- Quick actions are Find a Trade, Start a Trade and Search. “Ask Seller” is a seller-discovery action on a deferred surface. Intended.
- No “Recent activity” list. Home has no activity feed; activity exists per trade only. Intended.
- The five-step progress bars are not drawn. The platform does not map a status to a step, and a client-side mapping would invent one. Deviation.
- Past trades are a section the reference does not draw. The rescope asks for them, so home lists them. Intended.
- The attention and active counts are the person's real trades, not the reference's six.

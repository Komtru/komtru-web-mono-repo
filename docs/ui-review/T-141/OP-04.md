## Deviations

- **Alerts** are the two queue views the platform can answer: Escalated and Clock at risk, as two tables. The reference's mixed feed (payout hold, return window, unsealed return) and "Mark all read" need an alert inbox that does not exist.
- **Escalation rules.** The reference lists four rules (clock 80% used, clock runs out, money over 1M held, tampered parcel). Only rules the queue really applies are shown, in the same When / Then table: last quarter of the window, clock runs out, case escalated. The money and parcel rules belong to the settlement and custody tickets that enforce them.
- "Critical ones can't be switched off" copy is dropped: there is no notification preference to switch.

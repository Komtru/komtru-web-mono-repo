## Deviations

**Shell chrome and the stand-in.** The reference draws a full-screen mobile frame with its own top bar and pinned footer; the built screen sits in `AppShell` (brand row, mode switch, tab bar), `ScreenHeader` stands in for the drawn bar and the actions (`ActionStack`) sit in the page flow above the real tab bar, so the built shot is taller and every row below the header is offset. The shot walks the real screen in Chromium against `next dev`, with the platform's routes answered from canned data (TechEdge NG, iPhone 13 · 128GB), so names and amounts are illustrative. Amounts print with kobo (`₦195,000.00`) as the rest of the app does; the reference prints whole naira. The reference's `₦198,500` total is ₦195,000 + the ₦3,500 Buyer Protection Fee here, not ₦3,500 delivery.

**Real differences (intended, and why)**

- **One address, one state-driven screen.** TW-03 and CWT-S03 are two redraws of F-024's V1, `/trade/[id]/fund`, whose step is the platform's payment state. This shot is the state after a payment was started and not confirmed, where the reference's "Awaiting provider confirmation" banner shows; before an attempt exists the banner is not drawn (CWT-S03's shot). The reference's "Payment status" label above it is not drawn.
- **Methods are Bank transfer and Debit card or USSD.** The platform has no wallet, so "Supported wallet" is not offered. The fee card lists the trade reference, item price, Buyer Protection Fee and total, from the trade's own fee snapshot; the reference's "Trade expiry: 6 hours from creation" row is not drawn (owner question O1). The primary button is "Continue to payment" (the next step is the review, CWT-S04) with "Cancel" under it; the reference's "Refresh payment status" is "Check status" on CWT-S05.

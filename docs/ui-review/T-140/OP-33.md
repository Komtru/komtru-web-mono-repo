# OP-33 — My account (T-140)

- **Reference:** `docs/design-reference/Komtru — UI Living Master v2.html` line 9332 (`ops.komtru.com/me`)
- **Built:** `/me` in `apps/operations-app`
- **Method:** compared by reading the reference markup. The pixel-diff harness was not run: the page needs a STAFF session, and the platform has no `GET /admin/me` to give it one a fixture could stand in for.

## Deviations

- **Two of four panels.** Security and "What I can do" are drawn. **Trusted devices** and **My shift** are left out: the platform has no device list and no shift roster, and a panel of invented rows is worse than none.
- **Security rows.** Two-step sign-in reads "On, for every sign-in" (true of every console sign-in, since staff login always demands a second factor). Password age and the session count are not available; "Account" shows the username or public id instead. "Sign out other sessions" is not offered; Sign out is in the rail.
- **What I can do.** Role and the sections the role opens come from `GET /admin/me`. The reference's Tier and "Needs a second person" rows have no source and are left out. "See my privileges" (a link to OP-30, Roles and privileges) becomes an inline list of the permission codes.
- **Top bar.** The reference's search box, "press / to search" hint, authority chip, bell and avatar are the console-wide top bar, which no ticket owns yet; `ConsoleShell` has no slot for it. Left to T-141 with the Work Queue.
- **Back row** ("Team") is not drawn: My account is reached from the rail, not from a Team page.
- **Heading.** The page title is the shell's `h1` at 19px (`ConsoleShell`), where the reference draws 24px display type. ui-kit's decision, shared by every console screen.

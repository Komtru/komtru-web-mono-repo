**Shell chrome and the stand-in.** As in T-116: the built screen sits in `AppShell` (shot with `--content main`) and its actions are in the page flow above the real tab bar, where the reference pins them in a sticky footer; the tab bar and the moved buttons light the foot of every heat map. The shot ran against the real app and a canned stand-in for the platform (trade ids mapped to payment states, one proxy adding the sealed session cookie), so copy and layout are the real screen and the data is illustrative: the trade code, reference and timestamps differ from the drawing for that reason only.

**Real differences (intended, and why)**

- **No "Logistics fee" line, so the total is ₦458,780.00, not ₦462,280.00.** Pre-approved deviation C7 (Q5): no shipping price touches the platform. Every amount is the trade's own fee snapshot from the API; nothing is added up in the app.
- **"Buyer Protection Fee" without "(1.5%)".** The fee snapshot carries the amount but not the rate, and the app does not work a percentage out of two amounts. Intended; the owner may want the rate added to the trade view if the label matters.
- **No "Komtru Wallet" option.** Q3's default: the wallet is off and AB7 is F-025. No disabled row is drawn, by the app's rule of not drawing controls that do nothing.
- **Method descriptions reworded** ("A one-time account for this payment", "Instant, in Paystack's checkout") because C8 replaces the dedicated virtual account with Paystack pay-with-transfer.
- **"Debit card or USSD"** for "Debit card / USSD": a slash read aloud is noise; same option.

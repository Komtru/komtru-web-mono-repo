**Shell chrome and the stand-in.** As in T-116: the built screen sits in `AppShell` (shot with `--content main`) and its actions are in the page flow above the real tab bar, where the reference pins them in a sticky footer; the tab bar and the moved buttons light the foot of every heat map. The shot ran against the real app and a canned stand-in for the platform (trade ids mapped to payment states, one proxy adding the sealed session cookie), so copy and layout are the real screen and the data is illustrative: the trade code, reference and timestamps differ from the drawing for that reason only.

**Real differences (intended, and why)**

- **No account number, bank name or "Copy account" button.** Pre-approved deviation C8 (Q6): Paystack pay-with-transfer issues a one-time account inside Paystack's own checkout, so the app cannot print one. The card states the exact amount (the payment attempt's own, or the snapshot's total before one exists) and "Show account details" opens the checkout on the transfer channel only.
- **"I have sent the funds" appears only after the checkout has been opened once**, because before then there is no payment for the platform to verify.
- **The info banner drops "NDIC-insured partner bank".** Nothing in the specs states it, and money copy states only what the platform does: Komtru holds the money until the buyer confirms delivery.
- **The total differs (₦458,780.00)** for the logistics-fee reason in V1.
- **Address `/trade/[id]/fund?via=transfer`** rather than `/fund/transfer`; one route serves every step, because the step is the platform's payment state. The round back control steps to V1 inside the screen (a button, not a link), so the method chosen is kept.

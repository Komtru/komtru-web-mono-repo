**Real differences (intended, and why)**

- **"held in escrow" for "locked in escrow", and "TechEdge NG is told it's safe to dispatch"** for "has been notified": the seller's notification follows the trade's FUNDED transition, which the platform makes moments after the payment is confirmed; the wording does not claim it has already landed.

- **The reference is our charge reference (`fund-<attempt id>`)**, the one Paystack and support know the payment by, so it wraps to two lines where the drawing's short `TXN-…` fits on one.
- **"Back to trade timeline"** in sentence case.

**Shell chrome.** The reference draws these as full-screen mobile frames with their own top bar and a
pinned footer, and no tab bar. The built screens sit inside `AppShell`. The shot is taken with
`--content main`, so it starts at the screen's own content and the shell's brand row does not shift every
line; the tab bar still sits at the bottom of the compared area, and actions are in the page flow
(`ActionStack`) rather than `.btn-sticky`, because a pinned footer would sit on the tab bar. What the heat
map shows is therefore real differences in what each screen says and holds, listed below.

**Shot against a stub.** The shot ran against the real app with a canned stand-in for the backend (trade
ids mapped to states) because no backend runs in the review environment; copy and layout are the real
screens, the data is illustrative.

- **"Fund it within six hours of creation" and "Trade expires six hours after creation" are not stated**: the trade view carries no funding deadline, and the drawn figure is a policy the platform does not expose.
- **No "Continue to payment" button.** Funding is F-024; the screen says plainly that payment is not available in the app yet (buyer) or that the buyer will fund (seller). Open question Q-B.
- **"Agreed terms" rows are replaced by the item card and the trade's own fee snapshot**: item price, buyer protection fee and "You pay" for the buyer, "You receive" for the seller, straight from `fees`. The drawn "Payment status Not funded" is implied by the line above.
- **The agreement history** lists versions 1..n. Version numbers and the current proposer are from the API; earlier versions' proposers are inferred from the turn flipping on every counter (the platform returns only the current terms).
- Text is "Payment hasn't been made" where the drawing says "Payment is not yet confirmed": nothing is pending, so "not confirmed" would imply a payment in flight.

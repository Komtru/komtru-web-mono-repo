## Deviations

<!-- One line per deviation the heat map shows: what differs, where, and whether it is intended.
     Everything below this comment survives the next run; everything above it is regenerated. -->

**Shell chrome (every screen here).** The reference draws these as full-screen mobile frames with their own
top bar and a pinned footer, and no tab bar. The built screens sit inside `AppShell` (the brief says to use
it), so each carries the shell's brand row with the Buy/Sell switch above it and the tab bar below it, and its
own back/title row is drawn in the content. That is the largest part of every heat map and is intended. Actions
are in the page flow (`ActionStack`) rather than `.btn-sticky`, because a pinned footer would sit on the tab bar.

**Shot against a stub.** The harness shot ran against the real app with a canned stand-in for the backend
(trade ids mapped to states) because no backend runs in the review environment; copy and layout are the real
screens, the data is illustrative.

- **No listing exists to start from**, so the drawn item card, the seller strip, "Recent reviews" and "Message to seller (optional)" are absent. Listings (T-056) and reviews are not built, and no route stores a message. The built screen describes the item against the category's Trade Standard and names the other party, which is what the platform's create and send calls take. Open questions Q-D in the plan.
- **Added, not drawn:** "What are you doing?" (buying or selling, from T-115's note; hidden for a certified merchant, who only sells), the category picker, the item fields, the price field (with the buyer total or seller proceeds read from the preview), the saved-address picker with "Add a new address", seller proof upload, and the other party's lookup with its warnings.
- **Delivery address** is a choice among saved addresses (T-115: "an address picker over the user's saved addresses, with Add a new address inline"), not a free text field.
- Only the top of the form is in the shot; the screen scrolls.

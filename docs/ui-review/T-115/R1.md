## Deviations

**Shell chrome.** The reference draws these as full-screen mobile frames with their own top bar and a
pinned footer, and no tab bar. The built screens sit inside `AppShell`. The shot is taken with
`--content main`, so it starts at the screen's own content and the shell's brand row does not shift every
line; the tab bar still sits at the bottom of the compared area, and actions are in the page flow
(`ActionStack`) rather than `.btn-sticky`, because a pinned footer would sit on the tab bar. What the heat
map shows is therefore real differences in what each screen says and holds, listed below.

**Shot against a stub.** The shot ran against the real app with a canned stand-in for the backend (trade
ids mapped to states) because no backend runs in the review environment; copy and layout are the real
screens, the data is illustrative.

- **The "Identity-verified proposal reviewed by TrustOps" banner and the "Respond within 4 hours" strip are not drawn.** They describe the risk-hold clearance (F-021, post-MVP) and a response window; the trade view carries neither a review outcome nor an expiry. The built banner says only what the screen is for.
- **The buyer strip is drawn** from the trades list's `counterparty` (display name, else handle; initials from it). It carries no trade count or "verified buyer" line, which the platform does not give a counterparty display, and "No reviews yet" is not drawn (reviews are not built).
- **The item card shows the item the buyer described** ("Apple iPhone 13 Pro", the condition) in place of a listing, and no "1 in stock".
- **Terms are the trade's own**: price, inspection window (from the standard), delivery address, ships-from, version.
- **Added, not drawn:** the seller's "Ships from" address picker and proof upload with the locked-state banner, because accepting a trade as the seller needs both (T-061). "Accept trade request" stays disabled until both are present.
- **Actions** match the drawing and add "Propose a change" (TC-05); Decline opens a confirmation sheet asking for a reason, which the platform requires.

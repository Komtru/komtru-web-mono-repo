**Shell chrome.** The reference draws these as full-screen mobile frames with their own top bar and a
pinned footer, and no tab bar. The built screens sit inside `AppShell`. The shot is taken with
`--content main`, so it starts at the screen's own content and the shell's brand row does not shift every
line; the tab bar still sits at the bottom of the compared area, and actions are in the page flow
(`ActionStack`) rather than `.btn-sticky`, because a pinned footer would sit on the tab bar. What the heat
map shows is therefore real differences in what each screen says and holds, listed below.

**Shot against a stub.** The shot ran against the real app with a canned stand-in for the backend (trade
ids mapped to states) because no backend runs in the review environment; copy and layout are the real
screens, the data is illustrative.

- **The counterparty is drawn on every row** that has one (display name, else handle), from `GET /trades`. A row whose counterparty is not bound yet or whose account was closed draws none.
- **Chip labels** come from the trade's status: `Verification Needed` (held, in the info pair, as drawn), `Your answer`, `Sent`, `Invited`, `Terms agreed`, `Declined`, `Cancelled`, `Expired`. The reference's `Protected` is a funded-trade state and belongs to the trade workspace.
- **The subline counts "needs your attention · active"** over the loaded rows; the reference's "342 completed" needs a history count the list does not return. "Needs your attention" is the server's `attention`, never worked out in the client. Rows come from `GET /trades`, a page at a time, with "Show more".
- **A trade the server says is waiting on you has the indigo border.** Q1 draws the held proposal with it; the server does not mark a held proposal as waiting on the proposer, so here it has none.
- The reference's brand row has a bell with a dot; the shell's has the Buy/Sell switch.
- **Open question for the owner: should a held proposal claim the proposer's attention?** Q1 draws the held row with the attention border, but the server's `attention` is `null` for `UNDER_PROPOSAL_REVIEW`, and the client follows the server. Whether a held proposal asks something of the initiator is a product call, and the rule lives on the server (`attentionFor`).

## Deviations

- **Seller 360 is not built.** Fulfilment rate, dispute rate, proof quality, payout holds, recent issues and the "Help them succeed" outreach are post-certification seller health that no module holds yet (risk, disputes, media reviews, listings are unbuilt or deferred). Not drawn, rather than drawn with invented numbers. Owner decision: build Seller 360 as its own ticket once those feeds exist.
- **Applicant panel** stands in for the reference's header tiles: it carries what the platform can state (verification level, trade count and completed trades, member since), not fulfilment or dispute rates.
- **Header.** The reference's title and summary tiles become the shared `CaseLayout` header: case number, business name, type, applicant id and owner, with status, clock and priority chips. No search results back row; a back link to the queue instead.
- **Shell.** Console frame with the permission-filtered rail instead of the reference rail. "Merchant Applications (n)" carries the awaiting-review count in the label: `NavItem` has no badge slot and the ui-kit shell is under parity tests, so it was not extended.
- **Evidence.** Documents are viewable inline (PDF in a frame, photo as an image, "not available" when a file cannot be served), then the ui-kit `EvidenceMatrix` (identity level, CAC lookup, documents present, bank name-match) with Pass / Check / Gap words, then the status log and the Trust Trail.
- **Decision.** `DecisionPanel` as in OP-06, with three outcomes (approve, request changes by ticking documents and fields, reject). Each needs a reason **and a policy cited**, both typed by the reviewer; the advisory fills neither. Take-first ownership as OP-06.
- **Bank name-match** is read from the verdict on the applicant's payout account; the platform does not trigger it from the application (needs the plaintext account number, which M8 owns).
- Permission states (no `merchant.application.view`, view-only) and the decided and changes-requested states are not in the reference.

## Deviations

<!-- One line per deviation the heat map shows: what differs, where, and whether it is intended.
     Everything below this comment survives the next run; everything above it is regenerated. -->

1. **The left panel is the wrong pattern, deliberately.** The reference's "What we know" is
   `.evidence-item` — a 32px tone-coloured circle (risk / warning / info soft) beside a bold line and
   a `.ev-meta` caption, with a hairline between items. T-014 ships `EvidenceMatrix` (the `.p13-req`
   rows TO-PA2 uses) and `Timeline` (`.p0-timeline`); `.evidence-item` is a third shape the ticket
   does not name, so the story uses `Timeline`, whose indigo rail and 7px nodes replace the circles.
   This accounts for most of the heat in the left column. Deferred, not accidental.
2. **The policy cite joins its trailing clause with `—`, the reference with `—` here and `·` on
   TO-PA2.** N2 matches; TO-PA2 does not. `PolicyCite` takes one joiner; see `TO-PA2.md`.
3. **The right column is ~3px narrower.** `.case-layout` is `1.3fr 1fr` with a 20px gap on both
   sides; the remainder is sub-pixel rounding inside the 1040px frame.
4. **Body copy sits 1–2px lower per paragraph.** `ui-kit` ships no stylesheet, so there is no global
   `body { line-height }`; the reference sets one. T-017 owns that rule and closing this is its job —
   `.storybook/preview.css` carries the element defaults but not a body line-height.
5. **Everything else matches:** the case header (mono id, 17px subject, parties line, risk chip), the
   dashed-indigo advisory with its uppercase label and aperture icon, the `a human decides, always`
   divider with its two hairlines, the three stacked actions, the mono policy cite with its document
   icon, and the grey footnote outside the card.
